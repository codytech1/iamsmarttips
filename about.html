THE JOURNEY

How I got here.

2022

Started Creating Content

On May 9, 2022, I uploaded my very first YouTube video while I was still in school. At the time, my main focus was on my studies, so I wasn't creating consistently.

2022

Reached My First 1,000 Subscribers

In September 2022, I reached my first 1,000 YouTube subscribers, marking my first major step toward building a community through content.

2024

Reached 10,000 YouTube Subscribers

In 2024, I reached 10,000 YouTube subscribers, my first major milestone as a creator.

2025

Expanded Beyond YouTube

On April 1, 2025, I expanded beyond YouTube and started creating content on TikTok and Facebook, allowing me to reach and connect with a much larger audience.

2025

First Brand Partnership

In 2025, I landed my first brand partnership with Favex, marking an important milestone in my journey as a professional creator.

2025

Reached 200K+ Communities

Less than one year after expanding to TikTok and Facebook, I grew both communities to over 200,000 followers each through consistent content creation.

2026

Expanded to Instagram

On April 1, 2026, I launched my Instagram page to continue building my community across every major social platform.

2026

Reached 50,000 YouTube Subscribers

In 2026, I reached 50,000 subscribers on YouTube, another major milestone in my creator journey.

2026

First Smartphone Brand Partnership

In June 2026, I secured my first smartphone brand partnership with Xiaomi, marking another major milestone in my creator journey.

Today

Building the Community

Today, my community has grown to over 500,000 followers across YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram. I'm still creating content every week with one goal: making technology simple, practical, and accessible for everyone.
